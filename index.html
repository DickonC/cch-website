FAQ

Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we'll talk through what you need in a free initial chat and agree a clear proposal before any work begins. Try the calculator above to see what the task is costing you now.

How long does a project take?

Simple automations can be running within a week or two. Bigger internal tools usually take a few weeks. You'll get a clear timeline up front and see working progress along the way.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what we're here for. You just need to know your business; we handle the technical side and explain everything in plain English.

What happens if it breaks or our process changes?

We build things to be robust and well documented, so problems are rare and easy to fix. If your process changes, the software can be updated to match.

What about our data and security?

We keep your data in your own systems wherever possible, only ask for the access we need, and handle personal data in line with UK GDPR. We're happy to sign an NDA before we start.
